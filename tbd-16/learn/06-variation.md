## Planned outline

Each step can carry details that make it different from its neighbours:

| Field | What it does | Musical use |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `Cha` | Chance, 0–100% — how often this step actually fires | A hat that plays 70% of the time stops being a loop |
| `Rep.` | Repeat / ratchet count | Two or three fast hits on one step: rolls, fills, drill hats |
| `Len.` | Gate length in 1/64-step units | Short and clipped versus long and ringing |
| `Vel.` | Velocity | Accents and ghost notes, from [lesson 5](05-swing) |
| `Not` | Note | Pitch — matters most on tonal tracks, from [lesson 8](08-bass) |
| `RNot2` / `RNot3` | Two extra notes, relative to the first | Chords from a single step |
{: .dada-minimal-table }

Also planned:

- **Micro-timing** — nudging a step slightly early or late without changing the swing of everything else. This is how you make a hat sit "on top of" or "behind" the beat.
- **Pattern length is per track.** Set the hats to 15 steps while the kick stays at 16 and the two drift against each other, resetting every 15 bars. The cheapest polyrhythm on the device.
- **Play order** — Forward, Reverse, Ping-pong, Random jump, Random walk. Random walk on a hat track is an easy win.
- Where the balance sits: enough variation to stay alive, not so much that the beat stops being a beat.

The finished exercise will expose Chance, Repeat, and track length directly in
the virtual instrument, with an audible starting pattern and Reset.
