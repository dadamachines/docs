## Planned outline

- **Swing** — every second sixteenth arrives late. 0% is a machine, ~20% is hip-hop head-nod, ~40% is UK garage, ~55% approaches a triplet shuffle.
- Swing is set globally on the **Tempo** screen (`Swing %`), and can also be stored **per pattern** — so one track can swing while another stays straight.
- **Velocity** — not every hit is the same size. Accents on the downbeat, ghost notes between the snares.
- Why ghost notes work: they're below conscious attention but your body still hears them.
- Building a boom-bap beat: kick, snare on 5 and 13, ghost snares around them, swing at 20%.
- The finished exercise will let you combine global swing with louder accents and quieter ghost notes in one editable pattern.
