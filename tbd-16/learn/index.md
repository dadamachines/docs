# Learn

A step-by-step journey from your first kick drum to a finished track — with players you can hear right here, and the exact buttons to press on the device.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

You don't need a **tbd 16** to start. Every lesson opens with something you can play in the browser, so you can hear the idea before you build it. Then a **Now do it on your tbd 16** section gives you the exact button sequence.

## Learn or manual?

These lessons teach you how to *make music*. They deliberately don't list every parameter.

When you want the full reference — every knob on every machine, every function-button shortcut — that's what the rest of this manual is for. Start at [Sequencer](../sequencer/), [Sound & Tracks](../sound-and-tracks/) or [Machines](../machines/). In a hurry? [Quick Start](../getting-started/quickstart) gets you making noise in five minutes without the theory.

{: .note }
> The **tbd 16** is still in preview and its firmware is moving quickly. Lessons that cover a feature which hasn't reached a stable firmware build yet say so at the top.
